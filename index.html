01 / Support

Make yourself heard.

Start with your original words

  1. Open Sayso and allow microphone access.
  2. Select a supported language. Allow any required speech model download to finish.
  3. Tap Start, speak, then tap Stop. Choose Original to keep the transcript, or a writing style to refine it.

No words appearing?

Check microphone access in iPhone Settings, confirm that your speech language is available, and try the built-in microphone with Bluetooth disconnected. If the issue continues, email your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the error shown. Do not include private recordings or transcripts.

Rewriting is unavailable

Rewriting needs Apple Intelligence on a compatible iPhone and in a supported language. Original mode remains available for basic transcription when speech assets are ready. Sayso requires iOS 27 or later.

Use the Sayso keyboard

Enable Sayso in Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards. In Sayso, choose Send to keyboard for a finished result. Switch to the Sayso keyboard in your destination app and tap Insert. Full Access is not required for typing or inserting a result.

Dictate while using another app

Start Dictate in Another App inside Sayso, then return to your destination app. Stop or discard using the Live Activity. Recording sessions are limited to ten minutes. Keyboard Stop and Discard controls require Full Access.

02 / Privacy

Your words stay yours.

Privacy policy · Effective 8 September 2026

Sayso uses Apple's on-device speech technology and optionally Apple Intelligence to turn speech into text and refine it. No Sayso account is required. Sayso does not operate a server to receive your audio, transcripts, vocabulary, or rewritten text, and includes no advertising or third-party analytics.

Audio and processing

Microphone access provides recording and transcription. Sayso does not save microphone recordings. You may also select an audio file to transcribe. Language and model assets may require an internet download through Apple services before processing is available.

History and preferences

If you enable history, dictations are stored locally and may be included in your device backup. You can delete saved dictations in the app. Turning history off prevents new entries from being saved; existing entries remain until deleted. Preferences and vocabulary are also stored locally.

Keyboard, copying, and sharing

Send to keyboard places the selected result in storage shared with Sayso's keyboard. Results expire from insertion after ten minutes and can be cleared in Sayso. The keyboard does not collect text surrounding the cursor in another app. Full Access supports keyboard controls for a recording session started in Sayso; the keyboard itself does not record audio.

Copy places your chosen text on the local-device pasteboard. Share and keyboard insertion disclose text to your chosen destination, which handles it under its own privacy practices.

Diagnostics

Copy diagnostics explicitly copies technical metadata and recording counters to the local-device pasteboard. It does not include audio, transcripts, vocabulary, input-device names, or route identifiers. Sayso does not automatically send diagnostics to a server.

Support correspondence

If you email support, Ali Soliman receives your email address and the information you choose to share to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive recordings or transcripts. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by email.
